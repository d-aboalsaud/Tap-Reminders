Privacy Policy

Effective 9 July 2026

Tap. is built so that your data never has to leave your device. We run no servers, require no account, and collect nothing.

Your reminders and places

Everything you create in Tap. — reminders, saved places, settings — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded anywhere by the app. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location

Tap. uses your location for exactly one purpose: firing reminders when you arrive at or leave places you chose. This is done with Apple's geofencing, which runs on the device itself.

  • The app asks for “Always” location permission so location reminders work while the app is closed. This is required by how iOS geofencing works in the background.
  • Your location is processed entirely on your iPhone. It is never sent to us — we have no server to send it to.
  • You can revoke location access at any time in Settings; time-based reminders keep working without it.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice capture uses your microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into a reminder.

  • Audio is used only to produce the transcription; Tap. does not record or store your audio.
  • Depending on your device and iOS settings, Apple's speech recognition may process audio on-device or on Apple's servers, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Voice capture is optional — you can use text capture exclusively.

Notifications

All notifications are local notifications, scheduled and delivered by your iPhone itself. No push servers are involved.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of data — we never possess it in the first place.

Changes

If a future version of Tap. changes any of the above (for example, optional sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be clearly announced in the app.
